The planner is rwarp::view, compiled to WebAssembly: no I/O and no rendering, so any viewer can call it. A Zarr source is read for its metadata only (zarr.json: multiscales, the spatial: and proj: attributes); a COG's plan is then fetched in merged range requests and each screen tile warped by wxy-warp from the level its plan gave it. CRS through proj4rs. Sources must be served with CORS. wxylib/web; all examples.
