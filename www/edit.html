Editing canvas

A plain canvas for drawing and editing vector shapes to the decimal: snapping to vertices, segments, midpoints, intersections and a grid; locked distance, angle, x and y; parallel and perpendicular; a coordinate table beside the drawing; every edit an operation in a log. Untouched vertices are written back exactly as read.

Data

Frame and ground

EPSG codes or proj strings (proj4rs, in wasm). Edits happen in the edit CRS; longitude and latitude with no edit CRS get a local plane. Vertices you have not touched are still written back exactly as read.

Ellipsoid distances use the point scale factor (exact geodesic when angle and distance are both locked); true north applies the grid convergence. Geodesic edges are drawn and snapped as the curve between the vertices. The readout shows ground distance, true azimuth, k and convergence.

Tool

Snapping (S toggles)

Nudge brush (5)

A soft ball: drag it against the selected feature (or click a feature to start). The segments it touches are densified at the spacing, then pushed out of the ball, or smudged along with it; Shift swaps push and smudge, [ and ] resize. Spacing is in screen pixels, so zooming in traces finer detail. New vertices the stroke did not move are dropped, and each stroke is one undo.

Type D, A, X or Y, a value, then Enter to lock it; Shift with the letter toggles relative. Relative x and y count from the previous vertex, a relative angle turns from the previous segment.

Selected feature

Edits

Keys and mouse

1 to 5 tools. Drag with the right or middle button, or hold Space, to pan; wheel to zoom. F fits the data.
Edit: click a feature to select it, drag a vertex to move it, drag a midpoint marker or double-click a segment to insert a vertex, Delete removes the selected vertex (or the feature when no vertex is selected).
Draw: click to add vertices, Backspace drops the last, Enter or a click on the first vertex finishes, Esc cancels.
P perpendicular, P again parallel, then click a segment: the angle locks to it. B toggles bearing steps. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo.
